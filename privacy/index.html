Topped Off Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

The short version: your fuel log stays on your iPhone. Topped Off has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. We do not collect, see, sell or share any of your data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter (vehicles, fill-ups, prices, service records and reminders) is saved in a database inside the app on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in those backups. Apple handles those backups under your own settings and Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot access them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not connect to any server we run, and it contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code from us or any third party. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, photos or health data, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

The optional Topped Off Pro upgrade is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, email, payment details or Apple ID. Apple gives developers sales totals that do not identify you.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you log a fill-up with Siri or the Shortcuts app, the numbers you give (odometer, gallons, total) are passed to Topped Off on your device and saved like any other entry. Apple handles Siri requests under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reminders

Service reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No reminder information leaves the device.

Importing and exporting

Topped Off reads a file only when you choose one to import, and creates a file only when you choose to export. Where an exported file goes after that (Files, Mail, Messages and so on) is up to you.

Crash reports

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's Analytics settings, Apple may share anonymous crash and usage reports with us. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Topped Off is not directed to children under 13, and because it collects no data, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, optional iCloud sync between your own devices), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
