Importing from Fuelly

On fuelly.com, open your vehicle and choose Export to download a CSV. Save it to the Files app, then in Topped Off go to More > Import from Fuelly and pick the file. You'll see every row before anything is added: duplicates and broken rows start unchecked, and nothing is saved until you tap Import.

How MPG is worked out

MPG is calculated on full fill-ups: the miles since your last full fill divided by all the gallons pumped since then. A partial fill doesn't get its own MPG; its gallons roll into the next full tank. This is the same full-to-full method Fuelly uses, so an imported history carries straight over.

I forgot to log a fill-up

On the next fill-up, turn on Missed logging the previous fill. MPG restarts from that fill so one missing entry doesn't produce a wildly wrong tank.

Logging with Siri

With Topped Off Pro, say "Log fuel in Topped Off" and answer the odometer, gallons and total. Please only do this at the pump or once you're parked, never while driving.

Restoring your purchase

Topped Off Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID and shared with your Family Sharing group. On a new phone, go to More > Restore purchase.

Backing up your log

Your log is stored only on your iPhone and is included in your normal iCloud or computer backups. To keep your own copy, use More > Export CSV. Deleting the app deletes its data, so export first if you might want it back.
